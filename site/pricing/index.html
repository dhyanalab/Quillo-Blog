Questions

About paying.

Is the free version limited in any way?

No. There’s one version of Quillo, and it has every feature. The services are about doing the work for you, not unlocking anything.

Why aren’t there fixed prices?

Every blog is different: one person on a Raspberry Pi needs something very different from a team moving a large site. Tell us what you need, and we’ll send a price for exactly that.

Can I start free and ask for help later?

Yes. Many people start on their own and ask for hosting or support once the blog grows.

Do I need the commercial licence to run blogs for clients?

No. You can run unchanged Quillo for clients under the free licence. You only need the commercial licence to keep your changes private or to sell Quillo under your own brand.

Tell us about your blog.

Write to us with what you have in mind, and we’ll reply with a plan and a price.
