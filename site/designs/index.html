Colours

Six colour themes, light and dark.

Every theme has a light and a dark version, checked so text stays easy to read. Readers can switch, or you can keep the blog light only.

Copper & Slate

Cool slate greys with a warm copper accent. Calm and modern. The original.

Classic Blue

White pages, a confident blue and amber highlights. Clear and trustworthy.

Forest Green

Deep green with gold accents. Natural and distinctive.

Ocean Teal

Deep teal with a bright yellow highlight. Fresh and lively.

Signal Red

Charcoal and white with a sharp red accent. Punchy, like a newsroom.

Graphite & Yellow

Black, white and bold yellow. High contrast and confident.

Fonts

Five font pairs, served from your server.

Each pair sets the headings, the text and the code. Every font comes with Quillo, so pages stay fast and private.
